# EnCBAM — Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

This document outlines the strategic positioning, audience targeting, and packaging for the EnCBAM compliance operating system. All marketing assets, website content, feature developments, and user experiences must align with these guidelines.

---

## 1. Core Positioning
EnCBAM is a **white-label CBAM compliance operating system**. 
* **What we are**: Backend infrastructure that powers CBAM service providers.
* **What we are NOT**: We are not consultants, not reporting agencies, and not a simple self-serve SaaS tool for individual exporters.

**The Core Proposition**: 
> *"You handle the client relationship. We handle the entire CBAM compliance backend. You look like you built a CBAM practice. You actually just plugged into ours."*

---

## 2. Target Audience (Who We Sell To)
We sell directly to intermediaries and service providers who want to run or scale a CBAM practice for exporters:
* ESG and Sustainability Consultants
* CBAM Advisories
* Carbon Accounting Firms
* Trade, Customs, and Logistics Compliance Firms
* Professional Services Firms serving Indian and global exporters

> **Note on Exporters**: We do not actively target or prospect individual exporters. Inbound interest from exporters will be accommodated, but the main GTM engine is partner-focused (B2B2B).

---

## 3. Engagement Models
Partners can interact with and distribute EnCBAM through three primary models:

1. **Partner Plan**: 
   * Access the EnCBAM dashboard under the EnCBAM brand.
   * Manage multiple exporter client accounts from a single unified partner dashboard.
2. **White-Label**: 
   * Complete platform rebrand (logo, custom domain, themed client experience).
   * Powered invisibly by EnCBAM.
3. **Enterprise**: 
   * Designed for large advisory firms.
   * Custom deployments, full API access, and unlimited client support.

* **Entry Ramp**: Hesitant partners can start with a per-report pilot or revenue-share agreement, with a path to convert to standard recurring partner plans within two quarters.

---

## 4. Pricing Philosophy
We price as infrastructure, not as a standard seat-based SaaS.
* **No Public Pricing**: Pricing is opaque, and a public pricing page is omitted.
* **Custom Scoping**: Every conversation is individually scoped before a price is offered.
* **Value Metric**: Priced relative to the hiring/consultancy costs our infrastructure saves the partner firm.

---

## 5. Positioning Exclusions (What to Remove/Avoid)
To maintain our positioning as a premium enterprise infrastructure backend, do **NOT** use or showcase:
* **Self-serve signups** (e.g., "Sign up now", "Create a free account")
* **Free trials** (e.g., "Start 21-day free trial")
* **"For Exporters" messaging** as our primary headline or value proposition
* **Cheap per-seat or per-user pricing**
* Any language reducing EnCBAM to a simple tool/app rather than an operating system/infrastructure backend

---

## 6. Long-Term Vision
We are defining the category of **Infrastructure Backbone for CBAM Service Providers globally**. When a consulting or advisory firm decides to offer CBAM compliance services, EnCBAM should be the default integration choices.
